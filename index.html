The Best Movies According to Gio Friginal


My top 10 movies of all time

Titanic (1997)

Titanic is one of my top movies because it is emotional. Even though we know the ending, it still hurts to watch.
Jack and Rose feel like real people. Their love story is simple but touching. The movie slowly builds tension. When the ship starts sinking, everything becomes intense. The scenes feel realistic and scary.
The music adds to the sadness. It makes you feel for the characters. This movie stays in your mind.


Inception (2010)

This movie is interesting because it makes you think. The idea of entering dreams is very creative. Some parts are confusing but fun to understand. The action scenes are unique. The visuals are impressive. Each character has an important role. The story feels serious and deep. The ending makes you question what is real. It is better when rewatched. This movie is very memorable.


Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

This movie is in my top list because it feels emotional and exciting. Seeing different Spider-Men together was amazing. The story is not only about action. Peter faces real consequences for his choices. The villains were interesting and well-used. Some scenes were very sad. The action scenes were fun to watch. The movie balances humor and drama well. The ending felt bittersweet. It shows Spider-Man’s growth.


Forrest Gump (1994)

I like this movie because it feels simple and honest. Forrest is a kind and lovable character. The movie shows his life journey. Even small moments feel meaningful. The story moves smoothly through time. Some scenes are funny while others are sad. The movie teaches life lessons naturally. The music fits the mood well. It is easy to understand. This movie feels comforting.


Interstellar (2014)

This movie stands out because it mixes space and emotion. The space scenes look beautiful. The music makes the scenes more powerful. The relationship between the father and daughter is emotional. Time plays an important role in the story. Some scenes are sad and heavy. The movie feels serious and ambitious. It makes you think about life and time. The story feels unique. It leaves a strong impact.


The Shawshank Redemption (1994)

This movie is great because of its simple storytelling. The characters feel realistic. The theme of hope is strong. The prison setting feels believable. The story slowly builds over time. The friendship between characters feels real. The movie is calm but emotional. The ending is very satisfying. It makes you feel inspired. This movie is timeless.


Gladiator (2000)

This movie is in my top list because it is powerful. Maximus is a strong and relatable character. His story is about loss and revenge. The fight scenes are intense. The music makes the scenes more emotional. The movie shows honor and courage. The setting feels realistic. The story keeps you interested. The ending is emotional. It is a great historical movie.


Jurassic Park (1993)

This movie is amazing because it makes dinosaurs feel real. The sense of wonder is strong. The suspense keeps you on edge. The effects still look good today. The music is exciting. The characters react realistically. The movie mixes fear and fun well. Every scene feels important. It is enjoyable for all ages. This movie is a classic.
